🎉 AI Text Detector installed!

Two quick steps and you are ready to check your first passage.

1. Pin the extension

Click the puzzle icon in the toolbar and pin AI Text Detector so it is always one click away.

2. Check a passage

Select any text on a page, right-click it and choose Check with AI Text Detector. You get a score, a verdict and a sentence-by-sentence view — and the text never leaves your computer.
